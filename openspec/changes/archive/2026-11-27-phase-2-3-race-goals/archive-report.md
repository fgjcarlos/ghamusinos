## Where the contract lives

The archived `proposal.md` is the contract record of the phase. The code that
implements it:

- `internal/db/migrations/00018_races.sql` creates `races` and `race_tracks`
  and adds `planned_sessions.race_id`.
- `internal/races/store.go` holds the model and the associations;
  `internal/races/progress.go` computes the progress.
- `internal/http/handlers/races.go` mounts `/api/v1/races` on the authenticated
  v1 router through `MountRaces`: `GET` and `POST /races`, `GET`, `PUT` and
  `DELETE /races/{id}`, `GET /races/{id}/progress`, and `PUT` and `DELETE
  /races/{id}/tracks/{trackId}`.
- `web/src/features/races` is the `/carreras` screen.

Tests that cover it: `TestIntegration_RaceGoalsCRUDAndOwnership`
(`internal/app`), `TestRaceProgress` and `TestWorkoutRaceIDCreatePatchAndClear`
(`internal/http/handlers`), and `web/src/features/races/RacesPage.test.tsx`.

## Decisions and limitations

- The progress is orientative: it compares the volume registered over four
  weeks against the goals of the race. It is not a readiness percentage.
- Sessions are counted by the state persisted in phase 2.2; no state is
  inferred from an activity.
- The race plan generated by the GPX lab is still not persisted. #303 asked to
  assess storing it against the race, and #353 left it out of scope.
